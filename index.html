Who I am

        
I'm Jay — a software engineer with an AI specialization who likes owning a problem end to end: the model, the backend, and the interface a user actually touches. I co-founded Ayebot, a B2B SaaS platform that puts a private AI assistant inside universities, and pushed it to a signed pilot with a tier-1 private university.

        
I also write. My in-progress paper, OI-Bench, proposes a standard way to benchmark organoid-intelligence systems — an emerging "wetware computing" area that has no shared way to measure results yet.

        
Right now I'm at McGill for a Master of Management Analytics, adding the business and data side to the engineering. I like clean systems, honest documentation, and shipping over polishing forever.
